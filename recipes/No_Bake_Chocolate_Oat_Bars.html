No-Bake Chocolate Oat Bars

Delicious no-bake oatmeal bars with a chocolaty peanut butter layer.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter
  • ½ cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 3 cups quick-cooking oats
  • 1 cup semisweet chocolate chips
  • ½ cup peanut butter

Steps

  1. Grease a 9-inch square baking pan.
  2. Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium heat; stir in brown sugar and vanilla, then stir in oats. Cook over low heat until mixture is well blended, 2 to 3 minutes.
  3. Press half oat mixture into the bottom of the prepared pan. Reserve remaining oat mixture for topping.
  4. Meanwhile, melt chocolate chips and peanut butter in a small heavy saucepan over low heat, stirring frequently until smooth. Pour chocolate mixture over crust in the pan; spread evenly with a knife or the back of a spoon.
  5. Crumble remaining oat mixture over chocolate layer, pressing down gently. Cover and refrigerate for 2 to 3 hours or up to overnight. Bring to room temperature before cutting into bars.

Tip

These bars can be frozen; thaw 10 minutes before serving.
